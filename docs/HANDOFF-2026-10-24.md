## How the rules page stays honest

`lib/rules/catalogue.ts` holds the rules. Numbers come from exported constants. Each rule of kind `ai` carries
`prompt: { to, contains }`; `test:api` fails if that phrase (case-insensitive, must be on one line of the prompt) is
missing from the real prompt. When you change a prompt, change its rule in the catalogue in the same commit.
